The theme is Indian heritage, history and culture.

This is a playable game prototype, not just a UI mockup. All buttons, dice, player turns, character movement, videos, questions, scoring, settings and winning conditions must actually work.

5. HOW TO PLAY

The HOW TO PLAY button should open a clear rules/instructions panel.

Explain the game simply:

Objective

Travel through India's heritage journey by moving around the spiral board and reach the centre of the spiral.

Gameplay

Each player selects a historical character.

Players take turns rolling their dice.

The number rolled determines how many spaces the character moves.

Players move along the spiral path.

The board contains selected heritage destinations.

When a player reaches a heritage destination, a video about that place appears.

The player must watch the video for 30 seconds.

After the 30-second video experience, a question related to that historical place is presented.

If the answer is correct, the player receives 100 Heritage Points.

If the answer is incorrect, the player moves 2 spaces backwards.

Players continue taking turns.
The first player to reach the centre of the spiral wins.
